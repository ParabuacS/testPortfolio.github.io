The following are works that have either been unpublished or
are currently undergoing preparations to be shared at a later date.

Ode To Eternity

A fanfiction of Classroom of the Elite starring an original character admitted into the Advanced Nurturing High School as a Class A student. Follow her journey as she rediscovers herself in a meritocratic society of teenagers, aiming to get to the top. Will her anxious thoughts take her over, or will she percevere alongside her new classmates?



The Mysterious Heroes

A fanfiction of Classroom of the Elite, following the story of an original character enrolled as a Class D student. The boy seems to have a weird history, having connections to powerful figures without knowing it. Will those connections be his end, or his beginning?



Date A Live: Dimensional Walkers

A fanfiction of Date A Live, following a small group of people that found themselves trapped on Earth after a teleportation spell. In the eyes of the citizens and organizations, they were unknowns that needed to be taken out. However, a certain Worst Spirit might just help them out...



COTE: The Mysterious Siblings

A fanfiction of Classroom of the Elite that showcases the complicated relationship between two siblings, as well as the new difficulties they face alongside the other Class D students. There was a massive conspiracy happening due to the death of their mother, but that wasn't about to stop them from living out their best lives.
